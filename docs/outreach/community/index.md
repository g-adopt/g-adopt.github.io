# Community

G-ADOPT developers host regular face-to-face and online events designed to provide the
geoscientific community with a range of opportunities to interact on targeted model
development.

**Monthly clinic**

: A bring-your-own-problem clinic, where new and existing users can bring models,
issues, workflow questions, or early research ideas and get practical help. Runs
every first Thursday of the month at 3 pm AEDT; remote attendance via
[Zoom](LINK).

**Quarterly webinar**

: A thematic international webinar series, with each session focusing on a particular
application area, method, or scientific question. Runs in February, May, August, and
November, on the last Thursday of the month at 3 pm AEDT; remote attendance via
[Zoom](LINK).

**Annual workshop**

: A flagship workshop that brings the community together more formally. It combines
training with research presentations and user case studies. Usually held in October;
details announced in July.
