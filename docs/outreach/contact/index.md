# Contact

G-ADOPT offers various direct communication channels depending on your needs. Do not
hesitate to get in touch; we will happily come back to you.

**GitHub**

: G-ADOPT is developed publicly on [GitHub](https://github.com/g-adopt/g-adopt). For any
code-related queries, such as bug reports or feature requests, please raise an
[issue](https://github.com/g-adopt/g-adopt/issues) or open a
[discussion](https://github.com/g-adopt/g-adopt/discussions) on our project page.

**Slack**

: G-ADOPT's general communication channel is hosted on
[Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/g-adopt/shared_invite/zt-40awiwyqv-dZPtXRT9GCSA3k~mlfu5jQ).
By joining the channel, you can interact with project leaders and core developers to
discuss scientific ideas or model implementations, for example.

**Email**

: You can privately email the G-ADOPT project team at <help@gadopt.org>.
