# Outreach

G-ADOPT aims to provide the geoscientific community with a powerful and flexible
computational platform that allows the development of ambitious, yet user-friendly,
data-driven numerical models. As an open-source project, G-ADOPT reaches out to the
community in several ways to demonstrate novel capabilities, support existing and
prospective users, and attract new collaborators. Learn more about G-ADOPT's outreach
approach and engage with the people who maintain existing and develop upcoming
functionalities by attending regular events, exploring media coverage and scientific
contributions, and connecting with our direct communication channels.
